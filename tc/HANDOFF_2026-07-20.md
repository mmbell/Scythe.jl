## THE ROOT CAUSE, measured: the vortex is not a DISCRETE steady state

Every failure in this campaign — across vortex profiles, SSTs, barotropic-layer
settings, resolutions and timesteps — is one mechanism, and it is upstream of
every parameterization.

**Experiment** (`model_tests/tc_balance_holdtest.jl`, config `nophysics`: Louis
BL, surface fluxes and precipitation OFF; `Ls = K_min = Khdiff_heat =
Khdiff_water = 0`). Same RE87 initial condition, 6 h, nest1 surface:

    t[h]        0       1       2       3       4       5       6
    dp [hPa]  -4.194  -3.830  -3.606  -3.310  -2.911  -2.495  -2.038   51.4% filled
    v  [m/s]  13.749  13.387  13.107  12.812  12.573  12.311  11.965   13% lost

    (full physics, same window: 63.5% filled, 28% of wind lost)

In inviscid axisymmetric dynamics a gradient-wind + hydrostatically balanced
vortex **is** a steady solution. Losing half the pressure deficit in six hours
while the wind loses 13% means the initial state is nowhere near a discrete
steady state. Physics accounts for only the last ~12 percentage points.

**`KH_WATER` is exonerated.** The non-energy-conservative water mixing was the
leading suspect (newly enabled, documented as "it WILL drift energy"); it is not
the driver. Measuring its drift is still worth doing, but it is not this bug.

**Why this was never caught:** `balanced_vortex_fields` reports
`residual = 0.000358`, which is the ANALYTIC gradient-wind residual on the 500 m
radial WORK GRID. Nothing has ever measured the residual under the model's own
discrete operators on the model grid. That is the gap.

### The downstream chain — all one mechanism

    p' drains from t=0  ->  flow supergradient  ->  surface OUTFLOW at all radii
      ->  axis subsidence by mass continuity  ->  adiabatic BL warming ~1 K/h
      ->  T_axis 301 -> 309 K (air 7 K WARMER than SST)  ->  BL stabilises
      ->  no convection to ventilate it  ->  loop reinforces

Everything previous sessions chased separately — warm-core decay, reversed
air-sea fluxes, "pressure fills faster than the winds", supergradient outflow,
axis subsidence — are symptoms of this single drain. **Do not spend another
session tuning the vortex, the SST, or the sounding.** They cannot fix it.

### Recommended next step

Solve the balance in the model's own discrete operators (iterative nonlinear
balance, or a digital-filter initialization) instead of solving analytically on
a work grid and transferring. Before building that, run the cheap diagnostic:
**measure the discrete gradient-wind residual on the model grid** and attribute
the error to (a) the radial spectral fit, (b) the vertical mish, or (c) the
reference-state perturbation split. That tells you what to fix.

## What this session DID establish (all verified)

1. **The RE87 vortex fixes the symmetric instability.** `vortex_profile = :re87`
   (eq. 37) with `Z_BAROTROPIC = 0`, `v_m = 15`, `r_m = 82.5 km`,
   `r_0 = 800 km` gives **0 negative-PV points**. The run survived 19.9 h where
   the barotropic-BL vortex blew up at 6 h. Keep this.
2. **`SST_K = 302.65`** makes the air-sea disequilibrium positive at every
   radius at t=0 (+1.10 K at the axis, +2.99 K far field).
3. **Suite green at 7665/7665**, confirming the previously-unverified `re87_v`
   addition from the prior session.
4. **The vortex does intensify** once convection starts: `max v` 11.6 -> 20.3 m/s
   with the RMW contracting 110 -> 60 km, rain onset at 14 h. The dynamics are
   capable of spin-up; the initial state just decays out from under it.

## Corrections to the 2026-07-19 handoff

- **`tc_500m_sst27_reversedflux` is NOT "stable ~1 m/s, just decays".** `max|u|`
  bottoms at 0.55 at 6 h then climbs monotonically to **15.4 by 14 h**, with
  `v_max` recovering 17.9 -> 23.2. Do not use "max|u| ~ 1 m/s" as a pass
  criterion beyond ~6 h; even the good baseline fails it.
- **Read intensity from nest1, not nest2.** nest2's `max v` is pinned at its own
  inner edge (r = 130 km) at every time, because the true RMW (~60-110 km) is
  inside nest1. The 07-19 nest2 recommendation was for the `max|u|` blow-up
  signal only.

## Separate defects found, not chased

- **Spurious cooling aloft: -5.7 K at 19.5 km, -9.6 K at 23.5 km in 11 h.** In
  the sponge layer. Looks numerical. Contaminates the upper domain of any long
  integration.
- **`STATE_DEVIATION = 0.15` is badly violated.** The crash trace shows `rho_d`
  at **50% of reference** (r = 260 km, z = 12.9 km) oscillating on a 2-timestep
  period before going negative in nest3. The timestep advisory reports "margin
  1.59x" on the 0.15 assumption, so it is actively misleading, not merely
  conservative. Note this deviation may itself be downstream of the drain.

## Practical

`JULIA_NUM_THREADS` is NOT set by the 07-19 launch line, so `addprocs` gives
every nest worker ONE thread (~3 of 16 cores). `tc/scythe_tc.sbatch` does set it.
Measured scaling is weak regardless — 137 / 120 / 116 s for 1 / 2 / 4 threads
(1.18x at 4) — so more threads is not the throughput lever; tile decomposition
(`NEST_WORKERS`) would be. Actual rate: ~20-22x real time at coarse resolution.

    env JULIA_NUM_THREADS=4 SCYTHE_TC_OUTDIR=$PWD/tc/output/<tag> nohup \
      caffeinate -is julia --project=. tc/tc_run_axisym.jl 259200 --trace 4000 \
      > tc/output/<tag>.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &

Progress goes to `<outdir>/nest*/scythe_out.log`, NOT the redirected stdout file
(which stays empty until exit).
