# TC axisymmetric run — handoff, 2026-07-21

Supersedes `HANDOFF_2026-07-20.md`. That document's diagnosis — *the balanced
vortex is not a DISCRETE steady state* — is confirmed and unchanged. What is new
is **why**, measured rather than inferred, and it is not where that document
pointed.

---

## THE DOMINANT CAUSE: the vertical BOUNDARY CONDITION, not the initialization

`tc_boundary_conditions()` assigned `NeumannBC()` to every scalar at `BCB`/`BCT`.
Springsteel maps that to the cubic-B-spline **R1T1** condition
(`factory.jl:_bc_to_spline_dict`), which is a **hard constraint on the basis**: the
fitted spline is forced to have zero derivative at `z = 0` and `z = Z_TOP`.

A tropical cyclone violates that at the surface in the two places it matters most:

    hydrostatic balance   dp'/dz = -g rho_t' /= 0    <- that IS the surface deficit
    RE87 eq. (37)         dv/dz  = -V(r)/z_s  /= 0   <- the vortex decays with
                                                        height starting AT the ground

So `p'`, `rho_t'` and `v` were being projected into a space that cannot hold the
balanced state near the ground. The damage lands on the **first load**, inside
`load_initial_conditions!`'s `spectralTransform!`/`gridTransform!`, before a single
timestep is taken — which is why no amount of care in the analytic construction
could ever have fixed it.

BF02 / O01 / Straka never exposed this: their perturbations are interior bubbles,
where `d/dz ~ 0` at the walls happens to be true.

### Measured

`model_tests/tc_discrete_balance.jl` section 5. Max discrete hydrostatic residual
`-(dp'/dz + g rho_t')/rho_t` [m/s²] over each patch, radial BCs held natural so
only `BCB`/`BCT` varies:

                        nest 1     nest 2     nest 3
    NeumannBC           0.1035     0.04825    0.008255
    SecondDerivativeBC  0.004559   0.002096   0.0003509    23x better, STABLE
    NaturalBC           0.000684   0.000512   0.000157     151x better, UNSTABLE

The **gradient-wind** residual is untouched by any of these (~4.6e-5 m/s² in nest 1
under the model's own operators) — as it must be, since the vertical BC does not
enter the radial derivative.

### Why not NaturalBC, which is what the physics wants

It blows the model up. `tc_balance_holdtest.jl 0.1 nophysics` dies on a non-finite
spectral coefficient at **t = 41 s** with `p` unconstrained at the ground, **166 s**
with `rho_t`, and **21 s** with either unconstrained at the lid. `E_t`, `rho_d`,
`u` and `v` are each individually fine — it is specifically the **vertical acoustic
pair (p, rho_t)**.

The Galerkin acoustic solve (`_assemble_spline_matrix`, `src/semiimplicit.jl`) takes
Neumann as its **natural weak-form condition** and branches only on Dirichlet, so
leaving `p'`/`rho_t'` completely free at a wall desynchronizes the implicit and
explicit legs of the AI2* split there — the operator-consistency requirement that
`reference/rirk_vertical_solver.tex` is built around.

**`SecondDerivativeBC()` is the resolution**: it keeps one constraint on the basis
while leaving `dp'/dz` **free**, which is the degree of freedom hydrostatic balance
actually needs. This is what `tc/tc_init.jl` now ships (`w` stays `DirichletBC`,
`rho_r` stays `NaturalBC` at the ground for rain outflow).

### Everything else is small — do NOT lead with the initialization rewrite

Same script, section 2, nest 1, against a **767 Pa** deficit:

    radial interpolation from the 500 m work grid    0.0065 Pa
    spline fit + the always-on l_q = 2 smoothing     0.24   Pa

and the trapezoidal hydrostatic integration in `balanced_vortex_fields` is
internally consistent to round-off (section 4: the residual on an unconstrained,
`l_q = 0` fit is ~1e-4 m/s², four orders below the Neumann figure). Rebuilding the
initialization on the native spline operators (`IInttransform` and a radial spline
antiderivative, as planned) is a **refinement worth doing, not the fix**.

12 h nophysics hold test, nest 1 surface, with the fix:

    t[h]        0      1      2      3      4      5      6
      dp[hPa] -4.289 -2.907 -3.357 -2.996 -2.817 -3.118 -3.122
      v [m/s] 13.848 13.133 11.919 11.198 11.049 11.210 11.391
    t[h]        7      8      9     10     11     12
      dp[hPa] -3.139 -3.353 -3.379 -3.446 -3.564 -3.874     9.7% filled at 12 h
      v [m/s] 11.502 11.598 11.712 11.783 11.804 11.822    14.6% lost, still rising

**The 27.2% filled at 6 h was mid-adjustment, not a drain.** Both fields bottom at
hour 4 and then recover monotonically for the next eight hours — the deficit from
-2.82 back to -3.87 (only 9.7% below its initial -4.29), the wind from 11.05 back
to 11.82 and still climbing at hour 12. That is a single inertia-gravity
adjustment settling toward a balanced state, which is what a well-posed initial
condition is supposed to do.

Compare the pre-fix run over the same window: a monotonic drain to -2.038 hPa at
hour 6 that was still ACCELERATING downward when it ended. Nothing in it was
recovering.

Preserved: `tc/output/tc_holdtest_nophysics` (12 h, the fix),
`tc/output/tc_holdtest_nophysics_6h_d2bc` (its first 6 h, reproduced bit-for-bit),
`tc/output/tc_holdtest_nophysics_preNaturalBC` (the 07-20 baseline).

## What is left, in priority order

The vortex no longer drains, but the adjustment transient is large. Candidates for
shrinking it:

1. **The residual 4.6e-3 m/s² hydrostatic imbalance.** NaturalBC would give
   6.8e-4 — a further 7x — but needs the SI-solver boundary tension above resolved.
   That is a solver question (how the Galerkin weak form and the prognostic basis
   agree at a wall), not an initialization question. This is the direct lever.
2. **The amplitude of the adjustment itself.** Shedding ~35% of the deficit and
   ~20% of the wind before recovering is a large transient for a state that is
   meant to be balanced, and it contaminates the first several hours of any
   spin-up. A digital-filter initialization is the alternative to item 1.
3. **The native-operator initialization rebuild** (`IInttransform` + a radial
   spline antiderivative). Now correctly scoped as a refinement: worth ~1e-4 m/s²
   on the gradient-wind leg, which only becomes the binding term after item 1.
4. **Nesting, and the sponge** — neither has been isolated for this configuration.

**The `l_q` smoothing is largely exonerated.** Repeated fits applied to the t = 0
state of nest 1, with the R3X payload applied first, over 2000 passes:

    l_q      deficit lost        peak v lost
    2.0        2.5%              none (13.80194 -> 13.80816)
    1.0        0.5%              none
    0.5        0.05%             none

The decay is sub-linear (0.26% at 10 passes, 0.77% at 100, 1.9% at 1000), so
extrapolating `l_q = 2` to the 21600 steps of a 6 h run gives ~4-5% of the deficit
and nothing of the wind. Real but modest, and not the main remaining sink. Lowering
`l_q` in the vertical would recover it cheaply if wanted.

*(An earlier version of this probe reported ~8% of peak `v` lost at `l_q = 2`. That
was trap 2 below — no R3X payload, so the first pass zeroed the junction and the
"decay" was the junction artifact relaxing. The numbers above supersede it.)*

`tc_balance_holdtest.jl` has no restart path and always integrates from t = 0
(the 12 h run reproduces the 6 h one bit-for-bit, so they are directly comparable).
To resume instead, copy each `nest<i>/21600.0.jld2` to
`<dir>/nest<i>_tc_ics.jld2` and point `base.initial_conditions` at a `.jld2` name —
`load_initial_conditions!` branches on the extension.

## New tooling

`model_tests/tc_discrete_balance.jl` — the measurement `HANDOFF_2026-07-20.md`
asked for. Builds a real `ModelTile`, calls the equation set once, and reports the
gradient-wind and hydrostatic residuals under the model's own operators, with
attribution across radial interpolation / spline fit / `l_q` / vertical BC, an
`l_q` sweep, a vertical profile, and the controlled BC comparison.

### TWO TRAPS that fabricate large residuals

Check for both before believing any future variant of this measurement.

1. **`load_initial_conditions!` already runs `spectralTransform!`/`gridTransform!`**
   (`semiimplicit.jl:604`), so it returns the state AFTER the very fit you are
   trying to measure. Use `read_physical_grid` for the raw analytic mish values.
2. **A child patch carries an R3X `FixedBC` at its junction.** Fitting it without
   the parent's trio payload constrains the interface to ZERO and reports a
   ~345 Pa / 3.3e-2 m/s² residual that the real run never sees. The real run does an
   "Initial exchange (state at t = 0)" (`nesting.jl` ~697) before stepping; the
   diagnostic replays it in `load_all_patches`. Both of my first two readings of
   this problem were wrong because of these, in opposite directions.
