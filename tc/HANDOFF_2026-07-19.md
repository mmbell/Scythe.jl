## Where it stands: BLOCKED on a symmetrically unstable initial vortex

The last three runs all failed the same way and the cause is now **confirmed**,
not hypothesised.

`tc_params.jl` currently sets `Z_BAROTROPIC = 2.0e3`, which makes `v` constant
below 2 km and taper to zero at `V_TOP = 15 km`. That vortex is
**symmetrically/inertially unstable**: its dry Ertel PV goes negative over ~1350
work-grid points. The model then does the physically correct thing — it overturns
the unstable state with an exponentially growing circulation. That looks exactly
like a numerical blow-up but is not one.

    config                min Ertel PV     negative-PV points
    z_bt = 0              +1.33e-07        0        <- stable, ran 13+ h cleanly
    z_bt = 2 km           -4.81e-07        1352     <- blows up by 6 h

Growth signature (max|u| in nest2, m/s):

    t[h]     1      2      3      4      5      6
    z_bt=0   0.95   1.66   1.29   1.05   0.81   0.55    bounded
    linear   1.20   2.10   2.63   8.70  17.35  93.19    e-folding < 1 h
    smooth   1.41   2.41  14.56  56.71    --     --     WORSE (higher shear)

**A C2-smooth profile made it WORSE, which is the key evidence.** The first
hypothesis was that the piecewise-linear kink in `dv/dz` at `z_bt` seeded it (it
does put a +0.695 K/km spike into the temperature gradient). Replacing it with a
smootherstep removed the spike but grew *faster*, because smootherstep raises peak
`|dv/dz|` ~1.5x. So the driver is **the shear magnitude**, not the discontinuity:
compressing the vortex decay into 13 km instead of 15 km, with the decay
concentrated, drives PV negative.

This is why it was independent of timestep, appeared in all three nests, and was
worst in the coarse outer nests. The user's read was correct: **not a CFL error.**

### RESOLVED — the answer, found at the end of the session

The user pointed out that **RE87 and BR09 used a plain linear decay with height
and NO barotropic BL**, so they must have controlled the surface temperature some
other way. Chasing that produced the answer. Two things are wrong, and neither is
the vertical profile:

1. **The environmental air-sea disequilibrium is only +0.52 K.** The Dunion
   sounding's surface air is 299.63 K and SST is 300.15 K (27 C). That is the

2. **The vortex is absurdly broad.** With `RANKINE_ALPHA = 0.3`, v is still

RE87/BR09 needed no barotropic layer because their vortex was COMPACT and their
SST sat meaningfully above the environmental surface air temperature.

### RE87 equation (37) is IMPLEMENTED — use it

The user pointed to Rotunno & Emanuel (1987, JAS 44, 542-561) eq. (37), now in
`src/idealized.jl` as `re87_v(r, z; v_m, r_m, r_0, fcor, z_sponge)` and selectable
via `balanced_vortex_fields(...; vortex_profile = :re87, v_m, r_m, r_0)`:

    v(r,z) = (z_s - z)/z_s * { [ v_m^2 (r/r_m)^2 ( (2r_m/(r+r_m))^3
                                 - (2r_m/(r_0+r_m))^3 ) + f^2 r^2/4 ]^(1/2) - f r/2 }

Verified against the paper: with their control values (r_0 = 412.5 km,
r_m = 82.5 km, v_m = 15, f = 5e-5) it gives **max v = 12.94 m/s** (they state
~12) and v = 0 EXACTLY at r_0. The cubic falloff plus the exact outer zero is what
makes it compact — our modified-Rankine (alpha = 0.3) was still 12 m/s at
r = 1050 km.

RE87 report their vortex "leads to a temperature adjustment of at most ~0.6 K at
the vortex center". On our sounding eq. (37) gives **+1.56 K** — a 4.4x
improvement over the modified-Rankine's +6.91 K, but still above their 0.6 K.

**ALSO NOTE RE87 section 3:** they deliberately construct a "model-neutral"
initial sounding (Jordan mixed to conditional neutrality) so the storm develops
"purely through an air-sea interaction instability" and artificially intense
clouds do not erupt. That is the opposite of the CAPE-loaded states built in this
session, and is probably the right target: near-zero CAPE, spin-up by WISHE alone.
Their Fig. 3 shows v_max flat at ~12 m/s until t ~ 50 h, then rising to 40-50 m/s
by 100-150 h. **A 3-day run only just reaches the start of intensification.**

### PV vs the RE87 outer radius — measured

RE87's r_0 = 412.5 km is inertially MARGINAL at our latitude. A compact vortex has
strongly anticyclonic outer vorticity (`zeta ~ -v_m/(r_0 - r_m)`), and `f + zeta`
goes negative when f is small. Measured with eq. (37), v_m = 15, r_m = 82.5 km:

    f = 3.775e-5 (15N), r_0 = 412.5 km : negPV 4546  dT +1.56 K  SST-Tair -1.04 K
    f = 5.0e-5   (20N), r_0 = 412.5 km : negPV 3040  dT +1.49 K  SST-Tair -0.97 K
    f = 5.0e-5   (20N), r_0 = 600   km : negPV    0  dT +1.70 K  SST-Tair -1.18 K
    f = 3.775e-5 (15N), r_0 = 800   km : negPV    0  dT +1.91 K  SST-Tair -1.39 K

RE87 used f = 5e-5 (~20N); `F_COR` here is 3.775e-5 (15N). **Widening r_0 removes
the negative PV entirely.**

## What was FIXED this session (do not re-litigate; all verified)

### 1. Hydrostatic re-integration in `balanced_vortex_fields` (src/idealized.jl)

It rebuilt pressure by trapezoid on the **full** `rho_t*g` profile instead of the
perturbation, so it never reproduced the reference state's own spline hydrostatic
solve and the error accumulated downward. At the outer edge, where the vortex is
identically zero and the fields MUST equal the reference: **p was 4.6 hPa low and
T up to 12 K cold**. Since `q_v` holds its ambient profile and `rho_vs` is
exponentially T-sensitive, that -8.3 K bias at 16.8 km turned RH 0.32 into 1.09.

**This was the origin of the user's original complaint** (condensate layers near
the surface and model top, supersaturated points at initialization).

Fixed by integrating in perturbation form (`dp'/dz = -rho_t' g`, `p = pbar + p'`).
Far field now reduces to the reference bitwise; **supersaturated points 40881 -> 0**;
balance untouched (`dp/dr = dp'/dr`). Regression test added in
`test/test_idealized_init.jl` (`flds0.p == repeat(pbar, 1, nr)` with `Vmax = 0`).

**NOTE the user's original hypothesis was disproved:** T-from-EOS vs T-from-Newton
agree EXACTLY. With `rho_c = 0`, `M = p + rho_d*e_bf02 = (rho_d*Cpd + rho_v*Cpv)T`
and `Q_ss = rho_v - rho_vs(T,p)` makes `wfactor = 0`, so the analytic init is
already an exact fixed point of `retrieve_temperature`. The planned post-fit
reconciliation was correctly DROPPED: the residual is 4e-6 kg/m3 (q_c ~ 3e-9),
three orders too small to matter. **Do not rebuild it.**

### 2. Surface-flux sign reversal (the reason the vortex would not spin up)

The balanced vortex carries a LOW-LEVEL warm core (measured: peak **+3.9 K at
z = 1 km**, +3.12 K at the surface, zero at V_TOP — NOT a maximum aloft, and NOT a
maximum at the ground; both initial guesses were wrong, measure it). Combined with
`RH_BL = 0.90` on that too-warm air, at t = 0:

    SST - T_air    = -4.79 K      (air WARMER than the sea)
    q_s(SST) - q_a = -4.04 g/kg   (air MOISTER than saturation at SST)

so surface enthalpy and moisture fluxes were **NEGATIVE across the whole inner
core** — the ocean cooling and drying the BL instead of powering it, the inverse
of WISHE. `Z_BAROTROPIC = 2 km` flips these to **+1.87 K / +4.29 g/kg** while
preserving the vortex (deficit -26.3 -> -26.8 hPa) — but introduces the PV problem
above. **The flux diagnostic is the thing to preserve; the mechanism for getting
it is what needs redesigning.**

This one fault explained the whole observed chain: no destabilization -> no
convection -> no heat source -> warm core decays -> **surface pressure fills
FASTER than the winds** (86% of the deficit in 7 h vs 53% of the wind) -> flow
goes supergradient -> **surface OUTFLOW** -> axis subsidence by continuity.

### 3. Horizontal mixing gap (user-spotted)

Smagorinsky fed **momentum only** — `Khdiff_heat` was a separate constant set to
0.0, and horizontal water-species diffusion did not exist at all. So the
thermodynamic and water fields had NO horizontal mixing, on an axisymmetric grid
where there are no asymmetries to provide radial mixing.

Added, both opt-in via a negative-value sentinel (the `Cd < 0` convention) so all
other configs stay bitwise identical:
- `Khdiff_heat < 0` -> `K_smag / Pr_t`. Uses the existing dry-exact entropy
  Laplacian, no new derivation. Energy-clean.
- `Khdiff_water < 0` -> `K_smag / Sc_t` on `rho_w`, `rho_r`, `Q_ss`.
  **NOT ENERGY CONSISTENT** — diffuses water mass without the energy it carries.
  User approved as a TEMPORARY noise control on condition it be documented; it is
  flagged in three places. It WILL drift energy. **Its drift has never been
  measured** because no run has yet produced sustained precipitation — measure
  `conservation_drift` once one does, then decide whether the full moist
  product-rule form is worth deriving.

TC values raised toward the user's prior axisymmetric work (`Twoway_PV_mixing`
used length scales, not constant K: Ls_free 500 m, Ls_bl 2000 m, K_min_bl 1000):
`Ls` 200 -> 500 m, `K_min` 5 -> 25 m2/s.

### 4. Supersaturated stratosphere at coarse vertical resolution

At 1 km cells the spline cannot resolve the tropopause cold point and undershoots
to 187 K (vs 199 K at 300 m). The Dunion sounding's stratospheric `q_v` floor
(0.01 g/kg) is then far above saturation -> 33206 supersaturated points at 15-20 km.
Fixed with a global RH cap in `balanced_vortex_fields` (`RH_INIT_MAX = 0.98`),
applied at fixed `rho_t` and `p` so it is balance-preserving. It is a bitwise no-op
at resolutions that resolve the tropopause (fine grid max RH is 0.9353 < 0.98).

## Mistakes made this session — do not repeat

1. **NEVER `rm -rf` model output. `mv` it aside.** I deleted a working run the
   user was actively examining. See `feedback_never_delete_model_output`.
2. **Do not judge a run from a partial diagnostic sweep.** I called "no convection
   developing" from `rho_c` at t = 1 h and 2 h when the 3 h and 4 h outputs already
   existed and had reversed — and then took an irreversible action on it. Check
   every output time that exists before declaring failure. On a spin-up that
   legitimately takes days, a 2-point trend at hours 1-2 carries no information.
3. **Measure profiles; don't argue from the sign of a derivative.** My "warm core
   at the surface because dv/dz < 0 everywhere" was wrong reasoning, and the
   measured profile (peak at 1 km) matched neither my prediction nor the user's.
4. **A plausible mechanism is not a diagnosis.** The kink hypothesis was
   well-motivated, self-consistent, and wrong — the C2 fix made things worse. The
   PV calculation settled it in one shot. Reach for the decisive diagnostic
   earlier.
5. **Change one thing at a time.** Several runs varied resolution, timestep, SST
   and init together, and the results were confounded.
6. Recommending a change and then reversing it after a diagnostic contradicted it
   happened twice (per-nest timesteps, the SST reduction). Both traced to reasoning
   from mechanism instead of measuring first.

`RESTART_INTERVAL = 21600.0` (6-hourly JLD2 checkpoints), so a long integration can
be chained rather than restarted. This matters a lot given a ~2-day spin-up: get a
